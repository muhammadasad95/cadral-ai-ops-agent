Damaged or Faulty Items

Report within 48 hours

If an item arrives damaged please report it within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the item and the packaging.

What happens next

For damaged or faulty items we offer a free replacement or a full refund including delivery charges. You do not need to pay for return postage.
