Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Yes, we deliver to Ireland and most EU countries. See the International Delivery page for prices and times.

Can I change my delivery address?

Yes, while your order is still processing. Once it has shipped we cannot change the address, but the courier may let you redirect it using the tracking link.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, digital gift cards from £10 to £200. They are valid for 24 months.

How do I contact a person?

Ask our assistant to connect you to a human, email help@brightbasket.co.uk or call 0161 555 0142 during opening hours.
